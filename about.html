Complex things, said simply

Sonya is a technical PR specialist and a former journalist. Her work is taking complex products, such as climate control systems, sensors and automation, and explaining them in plain language that growers, editors and buyers understand on first reading.

She has run trade press, distributor outreach, CRM and analytics for an international greenhouse automation company, and before that built PR for brands in construction, design and architecture.

Told in green brings that work to companies that sell to growers, with one rule: no competitors of an existing client.

We work fast. Action starts straight after the first call, and nothing is stretched into long roadmaps before a single email goes out.

How we work

Four rules we keep with every client, because editors and distributors remember who wasted their time.

Written for one reader

Each pitch, article and outreach email is written for a specific editor or buyer. A text that worked for one title is never sent to another.

Facts checked against your team

Product terms, figures and case numbers are confirmed with your engineers before anything is published.

Real numbers in reports

We filter bot traffic out of analytics and report replies, placements and booked calls, not impressions.

Fast by default

Work starts after the first call, not after a month of planning. Warm replies reach your sales team the same day.
