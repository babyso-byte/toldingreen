Questions about prices

If your case is not covered here, ask on the call. We will not upsell a service you do not need.

What is included in the monthly fee?

All writing, research, pitching, follow-ups and reporting for the services you pick. Tools such as Apollo seats or CRM licences stay on your account and are paid by you.

Why is a market base paid once?

Building and cleaning a country base is a fixed job. After that it is yours to keep, and outreach runs on it month by month.

Do you guarantee coverage?

No one honest can. We guarantee the work: a checked press list, original texts and personal pitches. In-house, seven industry magazines agreed to publish or interview within about a month.

Which languages do you write in?

English by default. Spanish, Italian, Polish, Greek, Romanian and Vietnamese are available for articles, posts and outreach.

Can we start with one service?

Yes. Many clients start with a contact base for one market or two articles, then add PR or outreach once they see replies.

How do terms and discounts work?

Three months is the base rate. A single month costs 20% more, a 6-month term takes 7% off and a 12-month term 12%. Three or more monthly services take another 10% off. One-off work is not discounted.

What kind of contract do you use?

All work runs under a freelance service contract with a fixed scope, monthly fee and notice period. Prices on this page are estimates until the contract is signed.
